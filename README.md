## 🔥 Why this tool?

Sending offers one by one wastes hours. Generic bulk tools are messy, risky and blind — you don't know what happened until it's too late.

**WhatsApp Marketing Tool** gives you a clean control room: many accounts, smart message variety, safety controls, a live preview, statistics, and alerts on your phone. You set up the campaign once, press **Start**, and follow it from anywhere.

## ✨ What you get

### 👥 Multiple accounts, one window
- Run several WhatsApp accounts side by side, each in its own tab.
- Every account keeps its **own saved session** — scan the QR code once, never again.
- Rename, close or remove accounts any time.

### ✉️ Messages that never look identical
- **Customer names** inserted automatically.
- **Variants** like `{Hello|Hi|Dear customer}` — a different wording for every customer.
- A random **emoji line** and **decoration line** to make each message unique.
- Save and reuse your favorite **message templates**.

### 🖼️ Product images
- Attach up to **10 product images**.
- Choose **how many images** go to each customer (2, 3, 4, 5, 6…).
- Perfect for clothing stores, accessories, restaurants and any visual product.

### 🏷️ Customer groups
- Split your list: new customers, old customers, a city, VIPs…
- **Each group has its own message and its own images.**
- Assign groups while importing, or add a third column to your file.

### 🔀 A different message from each account *(optional)*
- Have **5 accounts? Send 5 different messages**, one from each account.

### 🔍 Number check — without sending anything
- Find out which numbers are really on WhatsApp.
- Delete the dead ones **before** the campaign and stop wasting attempts.

### 👀 Preview before you start
- See the message **exactly as your customer will receive it** — name, decoration, emoji and images.
- Know the **expected number of messages** and the **estimated duration** before pressing Start.

### 📊 Statistics dashboard
- Daily messages **per account** in a clean chart (7 / 14 / 30 days).
- Cards for today, last 7 days, total sent and dead numbers.
- Export the dead-numbers list in one click.

### 🔔 Alerts on your phone
- Get a **Telegram or WhatsApp notification** when a campaign finishes or an account stops.
- You choose the destination, and for WhatsApp you enter the number.
- No need to watch the screen.

### 🛡️ Built-in safety controls
- Random delay between messages
- Breaks between batches
- Daily limit per account
- Automatic **account monitoring** — a problem account is stopped and its numbers go back to the queue
- **Opt-out list** for people who asked to stop
- Skip customers who already got the same message

### 📈 Logs & reports
- Full daily logs and an Excel-ready **send report**.
- One button clears the history so you can launch a **new ad to the same customers**.

### 🎨 Modern design, two languages
- Sleek dark interface, rounded controls, smooth layout.
- **Arabic and English** — switch with one click, your choice is remembered.
